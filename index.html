隐私政策

最后更新：2024 年

📋 概述

我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明《火柴人人海大战》（以下简称"本游戏"）如何处理用户数据。

简而言之：我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

📦 我们收集的数据

本游戏不收集任何个人数据，包括但不限于：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话或其他身份信息
  • 位置信息
  • 设备标识符（IDFA、IDFV 等）
  • 使用行为或分析数据

💾 本地存储

游戏进度（关卡、金币、技能等级）仅保存在您的设备本地，通过 localStorage 存储。这些数据：

  • 不会上传至任何服务器
  • 不会与第三方共享
  • 仅供您个人使用
  • 可随时通过删除应用清除

📱 广告

本游戏目前包含模拟广告位（示例）。如未来接入真实广告 SDK（如 Google AdMob），将另行更新本隐私政策并通知用户。

👦 儿童隐私

本游戏适合全年龄段（App Store 评级 4+）。我们不向任何用户收集个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

🔄 政策更新

如本政策发生重大变更，我们将通过应用更新说明或本页面告知用户。继续使用本游戏即表示您接受最新政策。

Privacy Policy

Stickman Crowd Rush
Last updated: 2024

📋 Overview

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how Stickman Crowd Rush (the "Game") handles user data.

In short: we do not collect, upload, or sell any personal information.

📦 Data We Collect

This Game collects no personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email, phone, or other identity information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
  • Usage or analytics data

💾 Local Storage

Game progress (level, coins, skill levels) is saved locally on your device via localStorage. This data:

  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Is for your personal use only
  • Can be cleared at any time by deleting the app

📱 Advertising

The Game currently contains a placeholder ad slot (non-functional). If real ad SDKs (e.g. Google AdMob) are integrated in future updates, this policy will be updated and users will be notified.

👦 Children's Privacy

This Game is suitable for all ages (rated 4+ on the App Store). We do not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13.

🔄 Policy Updates

If this policy changes materially, we will notify users through app update notes or this page. Continued use of the Game constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
